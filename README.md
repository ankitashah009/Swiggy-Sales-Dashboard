# Swiggy Sales Dashboard — Excel

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive "Swiggy Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel".

The objective of the project was to transform raw sales data into an interactive visual dashboard that makes it easier to understand sales performance, identify trends, compare categories, and explore geographical patterns.

Instead of looking at thousands of individual records, the dashboard converts the data into meaningful visual insights that can support business-oriented decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify sales trends across different time periods
- Compare vegetarian and non-vegetarian sales
- Analyze sales by day of the week
- Compare sales across cities
- Examine sales distribution across ratings
- Understand geographical sales distribution across states
- Build an interactive and user-friendly Excel dashboard

## Visualizations Used

The dashboard uses multiple visualization techniques to present different aspects of the sales data.

### 1. Line Chart — Monthly Sales Trend
Used to visualize how sales change over time and identify trends in monthly performance.

Purpose:

### 2. Doughnut Chart — Veg vs Non-Veg Sales
Used to show the proportion of sales generated from vegetarian and non-vegetarian categories.

Purpose:
- Understand category contribution

- Present part-to-whole relationships


### 3. Bar Chart — Sales by Day
Displays sales performance across the days of the week.

Categories:
- Sunday
- Monday
- Tuesday
- Wednesday
- Thursday
- Friday
- Saturday

Purpose:
- Identify high-performing days
- Compare weekday and weekend performance
- Understand customer ordering patterns


### 4. Bar Chart — Sales by City
Used to compare sales performance across different cities.

Cities represented include:
- Kolkata
- Hyderabad
- Mumbai
- Lucknow
- Bengaluru
- and other locations in the dataset

Purpose:
- Identify high-performing cities
- Compare geographical markets
- Understand regional sales performance


### 5. Bar Chart — Sales by Rating
Used to examine the relationship between customer ratings and sales distribution.

Purpose:
- Compare sales across rating levels
- Understand customer rating patterns
- Explore potential relationships between ratings and sales


### 6. 3D Pie Chart — State-wise Sales Distribution
Used to visualize sales distribution across Indian states.
Purpose:
- Show geographical contribution to sales
- Identify important markets
- Understand state-level distribution

## Key Learning

The major learning from this project was that **a dashboard is more than a collection of charts**.

The real value comes from selecting the right visualization for the right business question.

For example:

- Line charts → *How are sales changing over time?*
- Bar charts → *Which category/location performs better?*
- Doughnut charts → *What contributes to total sales?*
- Slicers → *How does performance change when we filter the data?*

This project helped strengthen my understanding of **Excel-based business analytics, data visualization, dashboard design, and data-driven decision-making.**
